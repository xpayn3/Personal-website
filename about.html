About

Interaction & Product Designer

Ljubljana, Slovenia

I design how complex, technical things are understood and used — across interaction, motion, and 3D. From product interfaces to WebGL worlds you can touch and rotate in the browser, my focus is making the intricate feel clear, legible, and trustworthy.

My work lives at the intersection of design, animation, and code. I think in systems — states, flows, and feedback — and care as much about how something communicates and behaves as how it looks. Every interaction is intentional; every detail is considered.

Whether it's an interactive 3D showcase, a visualization that makes a complex process legible, or an experience that spans screen and physical space, I bring ideas to life in ways that feel intuitive and earn trust.
