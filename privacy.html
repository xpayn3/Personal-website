Privacy

What this site collects about you, why, and how to have it removed. Last updated 6 October 2026.

Who

This is the personal portfolio of Luka Grčar, based in Ljubljana, Slovenia. For anything on this page, write to luka.grcar@me.com.

Visits

The site uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are viewed. It records the pages you open, the kind of device and browser you use and your approximate location, and it sets cookies in your browser to tell visits apart. That information is processed by Google.

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages, which keeps server logs that include visitors' IP addresses.

Contact form

If you send a message through the contact form, your name, email address, company (if you give one) and message are delivered to my inbox by Web3Forms. I use them only to reply to you.

Other services

Fonts are loaded from Google Fonts and a few scripts from the jsDelivr network. As with any file your browser requests, those providers see your IP address when it does.

Your light or dark theme choice is saved in your own browser so the site remembers it. It is not sent anywhere.

Your choices

You can ask what I hold about you, have it corrected, or have it deleted. Email luka.grcar@me.com and I will sort it out.

To stop analytics cookies, block them in your browser settings or use a content blocker. The site works the same without them.
